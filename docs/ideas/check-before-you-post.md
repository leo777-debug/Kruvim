# Idea: Check before you post (understood, not misunderstood)

Saved 2026-10-04. A feature and mission idea for individuals: help people be understood by people who aren't
like them, before they press "post".

## The problem
People get hurt every day by words that came out wrong: a joke that offended a nationality, a headline that
sounded like it blamed a community, an ad that felt disrespectful during Ramadan, a post that started a
pile-on. The internet only deals with this after the damage (moderation, deleted comments, apologies,
cancelled careers). Nobody works on the moment before someone presses "post".

## What it does
Paste a caption, headline, script or post and get, in about 30 seconds:
- how it lands with 6-8 groups (understood / misread / hurt), with the reason for each
- one suggested fix that keeps your voice and your message

Kruvim shows and suggests; it never blocks, censors or lectures. The person always decides.

Examples:
- A creator's joke would hurt Filipino viewers; Kruvim suggests a version that keeps the humour.
- A journalist's headline reads as blaming Indian workers; Kruvim shows an equally strong but fair version.
- A café's Ramadan ad feels disrespectful to Emiratis; Kruvim shows why and a better version.
- A LinkedIn post after a layoff sounds bitter; Kruvim helps it sound like the person meant it.

## Why it matters
- About 200 nationalities live side by side in the UAE; misunderstanding is the root of a lot of hate.
- Fits the UAE's official focus on tolerance and coexistence: an opening for schools, universities, media
  and government partnerships.
- It is like Grammarly, which owns the moment before you hit send; Kruvim checks how words land with people
  instead of spelling.
- Pledge: never used to create propaganda, target groups or optimise outrage.

## How it fits the other ideas
- The forecast says what people are feeling tomorrow (the climate).
- The check says how your words land in that climate.
- Storm warnings are the public good.

## Product
1. A free "Check before you post" page (fast, cheap mode of the existing simulation).
2. A browser extension: a "Check with Kruvim" button next to the post box on X and LinkedIn.
3. Paid: deeper tests (video and reels, specific audiences, A/B versions) for creators and journalists.

## Honest risks
- "AI telling me what I can say": show and suggest only; never block.
- False alarms annoy people: warn only when the risk is real.
- Group predictions can stereotype: always explain why, and stay humble.
